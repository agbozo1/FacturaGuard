FacturaGuard

Your e-Factura, checked before ANAF sees it.

Every B2B invoice in Romania must pass ANAF's e-Factura checks. When one fails, the reason comes back as a short rule code. FacturaGuard finds the problems first, explains them in plain words and helps you fix them.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Drop your invoice: the e-Factura XML file, a PDF, or a scan or photo of it.

  2. 2

    Check

    It is checked against ANAF's own official rules, the same ones ANAF uses.

  3. 3

    Understand

    Each problem is explained in plain English or Romanian, next to the official rule.

  4. 4

    Fix

    A suggested fix, checked again before you see it. If something only you know is missing, it asks.

  5. 5

    Share

    Export the final XML, or send your accountant a clear summary.

✦

Still have a question? Ask the assistant. It answers from ANAF's official rules and websites, and shows you the source.

Who it is for

  • Freelancers and sole traders (PFA) who issue their own invoices
  • Owners of micro-enterprise SRLs who handle invoicing themselves
  • Foreign founders running a company in Romania
  • Anyone who relies on an accountant for every small fix and wants plain answers first

What it does not do

  • It does not send invoices to ANAF. You stay in control of that.
  • It does not replace your accountant. It helps you both save time.
  • It never lets AI guess whether an invoice is valid. ANAF's official rules decide; AI only explains.

Your data

  • Files are kept in memory for one hour and never saved.
  • Questions sent to web search have company IDs, IBANs and emails removed.
  • This demo uses made-up invoices only. Please do not upload real client data.

What powers it

  • ANAF's official e-Factura rules (CIUS-RO)
  • NVIDIA Nemotron models on Nebius Token Factory for explanations and fixes
  • Tavily search, limited to official Romanian government websites
